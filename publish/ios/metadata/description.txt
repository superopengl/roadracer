Road Racer 2026 is a simple, fast top-down arcade racer made by Leo, age 10.

Drive through five levels of escalating chaos — from Rookie to Impossible. Dodge cars, trucks, ambulances, police, UFOs, pedestrians, dogs, and even kangaroos. Crash too many times and you're done.

Earn tokens to buy paint colors, glow effects, brighter headlights, and turbo engines. Mix and match your ride in the garage between runs.

• Five hand-tuned difficulty levels
• Touch joystick + on-screen action buttons
• Shop & garage to customize your car
• No accounts, no ads, no data collection — just drive

Built with HTML5 Canvas. Vibe-coded by a 10-year-old. Have fun.
