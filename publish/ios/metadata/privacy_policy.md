# Privacy Policy — Road Racer 2026

**Effective:** 2026-05-03

Road Racer 2026 does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- No accounts, sign-ups, or logins.
- No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
- No network requests are made by the app during gameplay.
- All game state (tokens, owned items, equipped cosmetics) lives only on
  your device for the duration of the session and is reset when the app
  is closed.

If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated and the
release notes will say so.
