Hi, I'm Leo. I'm 10 and I made this game.

You drive a car down a road and try not to crash into stuff. Easy, right? Except the stuff is cars, big trucks, ambulances, police, dogs, kangaroos, people who walk into the road for no reason, and sometimes a UFO just shows up.

THE LEVELS
There are 5 levels: Rookie, Driver, Racer, Pro, and Impossible. Impossible is actually impossible. I made it that way on purpose. If you beat Impossible you're really, really good at this game.

THE SHOP
You earn tokens for finishing levels. You can spend them on:
- Different car colors. There's a stealth black one (it's cool).
- Glow effects under your car. Get the Rainbow one, it's the best.
- Speed upgrades. Turbo is FAST.
- Headlights in different colors. The rainbow headlights are also great.

THE GARAGE
This is where you switch your stuff around between runs. Mix and match. Make your car look however you want.

CRASHING
Different stuff hurts different amounts. Hitting a person is only 2 damage. Hitting a truck is 10. Hitting an ambulance is 20. Hitting a UFO is 25, because UFOs are heavy. You start with full health. Don't run out.

WHAT'S NOT IN THE GAME
No ads. Nothing to buy. No signing up. Nothing tracking you. You just play.

I hope you have fun!
